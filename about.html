About Us (අප ගැන)

Teach Me is an independent educational website designed to help Sri Lankan students prepare for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination. Students can attempt past-paper multiple-choice questions, receive automated marking, and review their practice performance.

Our aim is to make examination preparation easier to access and to improve the website based on student feedback. Practice scores are not official examination results.
