Last updated: 19 October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you register and complete the final survey, we may collect your name, district, O/L examination year, O/L and A/L mediums, intended A/L stream and Arts subjects, anticipated O/L results, practice-paper performance, rating, feedback, and WhatsApp number. Registration information is initially stored in your browser session; information submitted in the final survey may be saved in a Google Sheet.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to operate and improve the website, understand students’ learning needs, decide which papers or features to add, suggest relevant future educational options, provide requested examination results, address feedback, and maintain the service’s security and reliability.

3. Sharing and storage

Submitted data may be processed by Google (Google Sheets / Apps Script) and Cloudflare as service providers. We do not publish individual student records. We may use aggregated, non-identifying statistics to improve the service.

4. Retention and security

We limit access to student data and aim to keep it only as long as necessary for the purposes described. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices and requests

You can ask the operator to access, correct, or delete personal information, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Students under 18

Students under 18 should review this policy with a parent or guardian. Where required, permission from a parent or guardian must be obtained. We recommend collecting only data necessary to provide the service.
